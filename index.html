About Me

My name is Yu-Chen Huang (黄余晨). I was born in 2000 and am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Astronomy at the University of Science and Technology of China. I am interested in high-energy astrophysics, particularly fast radio bursts and pulsars. I focus on their propagation effects and their interactions with astrophysical plasmas.
